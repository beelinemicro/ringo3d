      The Story of RINGO

      RINGO didn’t start as a website. It started the way the best games do — with Dad, a board, two dice, and a pile of colored rings.

      He drew up a 5×5 grid, lettered the columns R‑I‑N‑G‑O, numbered the rows 1–5, and put a wild star on each die. The rules fit in a few sentences, but the games never went quietly: rings get stolen, corners get claimed, and the first one to five in a row — or all four corners — gets to shout “RINGO!” at the whole table.

      It’s been a family favorite ever since — the kind of game that comes out after dinner and doesn’t get put away after just one round.

      RINGO 3D takes Dad’s board into the third dimension: five layers of glass stacked into a cube, a third die for the layer, and the one thing a wooden board could never do — a Rubik’s-style twist that swings whole slices of rings into new lines. Same rules, same dice, same shout — now with a hundred and nine ways to line up five.

        How to Play RINGO 3D

        • The board is a 5×5×5 cube. Columns are lettered R-I-N-G-O, rows are numbered 1-5, and the five layers from front to back are Sky, Violet, Mint, Peach and Rose.
        • On your turn, roll three dice — a letter, a number and a layer color — and place a ring of your color in that space of the cube. Each die also has a ★ wild face: pick that part yourself. Three wilds? Anywhere at all.
        • A wild can also replace an opponent’s ring on any space it reaches. If your rolled space holds an opponent’s ring, choose: steal the spot or roll again. Your own ring? Just roll again.
        • Or, instead of rolling, twist the cube: turn any layer, column or row a quarter turn, Rubik’s-style. The rings in that slice swing into new spaces — and new lines. You can’t simply undo the twist just made.
        • Two ways to win: five rings in a straight line anywhere through the cube — along a row, a column, front-to-back, diagonally across a layer, or corner to corner through the middle — or all four corners of any outer face. Either way, shout “RINGO!”
        • A twist can finish a line too. Whoever’s rings line up wins, even if the other player did the twisting. Finish more than one line at once for a legendary DOUBLE or TRIPLE RINGO.
        • Drag the cube to spin it. Tap a layer chip to see inside, or the explode button to fan the layers out.
